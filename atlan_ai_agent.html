GOVERNANCE AI

One governed control loop for Power BI KPI changes and GCP BigQuery changes: detect, understand, approve, propagate, validate, and audit.

End-to-End Overview

One simple flow from “something changed” to “everything is consistent again.”

Atlan supplies trusted metadata, history, owners, and lineage. The AI agent explains what changed and what it impacts. A business or data owner approves material changes. Every affected Power BI or BigQuery asset is then tracked until the update is correct.

5

6

Approve or reject

8

Workflow starts for every affected asset

Update linked downstream assets

Recheck, certify & monitor

AI rechecks the updated assets against the approved standard. Pass, pending, and failed results stay visible in Monitoring & Audit.

Simple way to explain it: Atlan tells us what exists, what changed, who owns it, and where it is used. AI tells us what the change means, what must be updated, and whether each update is correct. A human approves material changes, and responsible teams carry out the governed updates. Power BI/Fabric and BigQuery remain the systems where technical changes are made.

Downstream Update & Validation

Approval is not the end: every dependent asset must be updated and checked.

After an approved change, the agent uses Atlan lineage to open tracked work for every affected dashboard, semantic model, BigQuery view/table, or SQL dependency. It then verifies each update independently.

Approved → Propagated → Verified

1. Register the approved standard

The approved KPI definition, schema decision, SQL logic, or remediation outcome becomes the governed reference in Atlan.

2. Find every downstream asset that must react

Atlan lineage returns the dependency list: BigQuery tables and views → semantic models → reports/pages → dashboards.

3. Launch one workflow for each affected asset

Each dashboard, model, table, or view receives its own update task with owner, expected change, target status, and validation rule.

4. Apply changes through controlled engineering processes

Power BI updates move through development/test and Fabric controls. BigQuery updates move through the approved SQL/data-engineering process.

5. AI waits for the updated asset and checks it

The agent re-reads the asset and verifies that the expected DAX, schema, SQL logic, or data-quality condition is actually correct.

6. Track every item separately

If 12 dashboards are affected, Monitoring & Audit can show 9 passed, 2 pending, and 1 failed: not just one overall “approved” status.

7. Failed updates stay open

A failed asset is routed back to its owner, corrected, and checked again. The overall change is not closed early.

8. Atlan re-crawls and the change closes only when consistency is restored

Once all required assets pass, the new state is certified and the final record is written into Monitoring & Audit.

BigQuery has two valid outcomes: if the source change was approved, downstream objects are updated to the new standard. If it was an unwanted data-loss event, the workflow can require restore/reload and validation before dependent assets are cleared.
